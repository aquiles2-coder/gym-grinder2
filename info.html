How to log similar exercises and how to convert weight when you train one side at a time instead of both (or the other way around).

Similar exercises

There are far more exercises than the list in the app can hold. Similar movements can be grouped so you can still log them against the closest exercise name.

Pick the listed exercise that matches the same movement pattern and the same main muscles. Then log weight and reps using the 1-arm / 2-arm (or 1-leg / 2-leg) rule below when the listed name and what you actually did do not use the same number of limbs.

1 or 2 arms / legs

When the exercise name says 2 arms/legs and you do a similar exercise with just 1 arm/leg at a time, the weight you put in for each arm/leg must be doubled in the exercise log. The number of reps is the number of reps for each arm (or each leg).

Example: you use a 10 kg dumbbell and do 8 reps each arm, on an exercise that is listed as 2 arms/legs. Log 20 kg and 8 reps.

When the listed exercise says 1 arm/leg and you use 2 arms/legs, halve the weight and keep the same number of reps you did.

Example: you use a 20 kg bar and do 8 reps, on an exercise that is listed as 1 arm/leg. Log 10 kg and 8 reps.
